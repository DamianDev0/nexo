# Voice

Spanish first, **tú** never **usted**, no regional slang. Verbs first:
"Mover negocio a Negociación". Colombian numerals (`$ 38.900.000`,
`900.412.887-3`).

Errors say what happened, what it means for the user's data and what to do
next — never a code, never "algo salió mal".

## Say / Not

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "Mover negocio a Negociación" | "Update deal stage entity" |
| "Nadie ha hecho seguimiento en 12 días" | "Engagement metrics below threshold" |
